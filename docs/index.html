Edward R. Hyde

American civil engineer, West Point graduate, and long-serving dean who shaped the early physical campus of engineering at the University of the Philippines.

Biography

Edward R. Hyde was an American civil engineer who taught at West Point before joining the University of the Philippines in 1920. He later became Dean of the College of Engineering, serving from 1926 to 1940.

Campus contributions

  • Designed the engineering building that anchored early UP engineering instruction.
  • Created the floral sundial, a lasting campus landmark associated with the college.
  • Guided the college through a formative period of growth in faculty, facilities, and curriculum.

Legacy

Hyde’s tenure is remembered for connecting American engineering practice with the emerging Philippine university system—leaving both buildings and academic culture that later generations of UP engineers inherited.
