✨ Awesome Facts About Golden Retrievers

🏅

Super Smart!

Golden Retrievers are the 4th most intelligent dog breed. They're easy to train and love learning new tricks!

❤️

Big Hearts

They're one of the most loyal and loving breeds. Golden Retrievers make incredible family pets and best friends!

🏊

Water Lovers

Golden Retrievers LOVE water! Their water-resistant coat and webbed paws make them amazing swimmers.

🌟

Naturally Friendly

They're one of the most popular breeds worldwide. Golden Retrievers rarely meet a stranger they don't like!

🦴

Lifetime: 10-12 Years

Golden Retrievers live for about 10 to 12 years. That's a whole lot of tail wags and cuddles!

🌍

Originated in Scotland

These beautiful pups were bred in Scotland during the 1800s. They were originally bred for hunting and retrieving!
