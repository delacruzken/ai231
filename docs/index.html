Game Spotlights

Three distinct experiences, each bringing a different style of intensity and immersion.

Open World

Grand Theft Auto VI

Grand Theft Auto VI returns players to the sun-soaked, neon-charged state of Leonida, with Vice City at its center. The game follows the intertwined story of Lucia and Jason as they navigate crime, ambition, and survival in a sprawling modern world built for exploration, chaos, and unforgettable set pieces.

Key Features

  • Expansive open world inspired by Vice City and the broader Leonida region.
  • Story-driven experience centered on dual protagonists Lucia and Jason.
  • Dense urban streets, beaches, and wild areas designed for emergent gameplay.
  • High-energy action, driving, and mission variety in Rockstar's signature style.
Survival Horror

Resident Evil Requiem

Resident Evil Requiem leans into the franchise's legacy of dread, mystery, and carefully paced terror. Players can expect an unsettling journey where every resource matters, every corridor feels dangerous, and the unknown waits just beyond the next door.

Key Features

  • Atmospheric survival horror focused on tension, exploration, and suspense.
  • Resource management that rewards planning and careful decision-making.
  • Haunting environments designed to keep players alert and uncertain.
  • Encounters that emphasize vulnerability, strategy, and memorable scares.
Action Adventure

Marvel's Wolverine

Marvel's Wolverine promises a gritty, character-driven action adventure starring Logan. Built around close-quarters intensity and emotional storytelling, the game aims to capture Wolverine's ferocity, resilience, and complicated humanity.

Key Features

  • Visceral combat centered on Wolverine's speed, strength, and claws.
  • Narrative-driven adventure that explores Logan's character and conflicts.
  • Cinematic presentation with a darker, grounded superhero tone.
  • Action set pieces balanced with moments of story and atmosphere.
