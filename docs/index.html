Candle Types

Paraffin Candles

Traditional and affordable, paraffin candles are made from petroleum by-products. They hold color and scent well, making them popular for decorative and aromatic purposes.

Soy Candles

Made from soybean oil, soy candles are eco-friendly and renewable. They burn longer and cleaner than paraffin, with a softer fragrance throw perfect for natural scents.

Beeswax Candles

Premium and natural, beeswax candles burn the longest and brightest. They produce a natural honey scent and purify the air by releasing negative ions.

Gel Candles

Made from mineral oil and resin, gel candles are transparent and allow for creative decorating. They hold color vividly and are great for embedding decorative elements.

Benefits of Candles

Ambiance & Mood

Candles create a warm, inviting atmosphere that instantly enhances the mood of any room. Perfect for relaxation, meditation, or romantic evenings.

Natural Fragrance

Scented candles release pleasant aromas that naturally freshen your space without harsh chemicals. Essential oil candles provide aromatherapy benefits.

Energy Savings

Use candles to reduce reliance on electric lighting. They provide gentle, natural light that's perfect for creating comfort during power outages.

Stress Relief

The soft glow and calming scents of candles help reduce stress and anxiety. Many people use them as part of their self-care and wellness routines.

Home Décor

Candles add beauty and elegance to any space. Available in countless colors, shapes, and sizes, they complement any interior design style.

Mindfulness

Watching a candle flame encourages mindfulness and presence. The flickering light naturally draws focus and promotes relaxation and contemplation.

Candle Care Tips

  • Trim the wick: Keep the wick trimmed to ¼ inch to prevent excessive smoking and ensure an even burn.
  • First burn matters: Allow the candle to burn long enough for the entire surface to melt on the first use to prevent tunneling.
  • Avoid drafts: Keep candles away from fans, open windows, and air conditioning to ensure even burning.
  • Use a candle warmer: Consider using a candle warmer instead of a flame for safer, longer-lasting candles.
  • Store properly: Keep candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight to prevent discoloration and warping.
  • Never leave unattended: Always keep a burning candle in sight and never fall asleep with one lit.
  • Extinguish safely: Use a candle snuffer to extinguish candles gently, avoiding wax splatter.
  • Remove debris: Keep the top of candles free from dust and debris by gently wiping before lighting.
