Grand Theft Auto VI

Setting: Vice City and the state of Leonida · Platforms: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S

Grand Theft Auto VI brings Rockstar’s blockbuster crime saga to a modern-day Vice City, following the intertwined story of Lucia and Jason. Set across the vibrant, dangerous state of Leonida, the game aims to deliver a massive open world filled with story-driven missions, dynamic encounters, and the series’ signature satire.

Key features

  • Dual-protagonist narrative centered on Lucia and Jason.
  • Expansive open world spanning Vice City and surrounding Leonida regions.
  • Next-generation visuals, animation, and world density on current-gen consoles.
  • A mix of cinematic heists, free-roam exploration, and emergent activities.

Survival horror evolves
Survival Horror Action Capcom

Resident Evil Requiem

Genre focus: Tension, investigation, and combat · Platforms: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC

Resident Evil Requiem continues Capcom’s acclaimed horror legacy with a new nightmare built around atmosphere, mystery, and resourceful survival. Blending investigation with intense encounters, it challenges players to manage limited supplies, read dangerous spaces, and uncover the truth behind its unfolding biohazard threat.

Key features

  • Survival-horror gameplay balancing exploration, puzzles, and combat.
  • Tense atmosphere with an emphasis on dread, pacing, and environmental storytelling.
  • Strategic resource management where every decision can matter.
  • Modern presentation powered by Capcom’s RE Engine technology.
Marvel’s Wolverine
Unleash the berserker
Action-Adventure Super Hero Insomniac Games

Marvel’s Wolverine

Hero: Logan / Wolverine · Platform: PlayStation 5

Marvel’s Wolverine is Insomniac Games’ standalone action-adventure starring Logan, one of Marvel’s most complex and formidable heroes. Designed to capture Wolverine’s raw intensity, the game promises hard-hitting combat, a character-driven story, and a darker tone that reflects the edge of the adamantium-clawed mutant.

Key features

  • Original, character-focused story built around Logan’s identity and past.
  • Visceral, close-quarters combat that channels Wolverine’s ferocity.
  • Cinematic action-adventure design from the studio behind Marvel’s Spider-Man.
  • A mature tone aimed at capturing the grit of the character.
