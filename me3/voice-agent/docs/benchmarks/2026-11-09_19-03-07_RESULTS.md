## 4. Interpretation / next fixes

1. **Latency:** Mean E2E is near the 4 s course target, but p95 and LLM TTFT are far over — Haiku TTFT dominates; TTS p95 spikes on a few turns (e.g. interruption_3).
2. **Suspicious 0.00 s replies:** `e2e_heard` is almost always 0 — strong sign of **carry-over / premature audio**, which also inflates “pass” rates on stage trials. Trial reset / playout clear needs hardening before trusting sub-second E2E.
3. **Turn-taking policy:** Plain VAD path answers non-addressed speech (false TT 100%) and barges into mid-utterance pauses (TOR 1). Needs stronger endpointing / addressed-speech / pause tolerance.
4. **Interruption:** v1.5 interruption kept talking; barge-in course metric empty (`n=0`) despite `interrupted` marks — wire stop timing into the scored barge-in path.
5. **WER / ASR final empty in scored table:** transcripts *were* marked (`asr_final` ×48 with text). Offline corpus WER ≈ **22.7%** (target &lt; 15%). Official column stayed empty because silence commits fired non-speculative `llm_request` before `asr_final`. **Fixed in code:** early commits now use `speculative=True` (confirm/restart on `asr_final`) — next bank must show `course.wer` vs target.
6. **Hardware target:** Re-run on Pi 5 + M1A cables for the numbers that go on the course slide.
